## Security Outcome

- Replay custom nation names and flags are validated on write.
- Unsafe legacy replay identities are sanitized before public delivery.
- Client replay names and layers are normalized before rendering.
- Notices render title and detail as text rather than HTML.
- Client-supplied bot participation no longer mutates user metadata.
- Save kinds are immutable and Autosave is reserved to one managed saved-game slot.
- Per-user locking, InnoDB migration, transactions, and hard eviction limits prevent sequential and concurrent quota bypass.
