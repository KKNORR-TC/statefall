## Outcome

- Closes the public replay stored-XSS path with server validation, legacy sanitization, client normalization, and safe notice rendering.
- Prevents client-supplied bot participation from mutating other users' metadata.
- Enforces save/replay quotas under sequential and concurrent requests with an InnoDB migration, per-user locking, transactions, immutable kinds, and a managed Autosave slot.
- Implements the adopted product policy that site scores, rankings, and score-derived achievements are community-submitted and not independently verified.
- Adds `verified=false` to score API records and submission responses.
