Two distinct short bugle cues: a restrained salute for neutral territory, a brisk flourish for enemy territory. Completed territories only, never individual tiles. Taps takes priority when an enemy is eliminated. Both lower the music automatically, then restore it.

This is a listening mockup. In the game, battle intensity and outcomes must follow actual attack state. Hidden battles stay silent.

The music file stays on your computer. No track is uploaded or fetched from the live game.

What matters most

1. Ground attacks: clear order feedback; a living mix while fighting; restrained stalled and failed endings; fanfare for completed territory captures and Taps for enemy elimination.

2. Construction: brief worksite ambience at the start and a separate signature for every building type. Instant buildings should not play two cues together.

3. Missile defense: strategic launch, fast SAM launch, brief successful intercept, and unmistakable nuclear impact.

4. Ownership: your actions foregrounded, threats to you prioritized, visible bot battles distant and restrained.
